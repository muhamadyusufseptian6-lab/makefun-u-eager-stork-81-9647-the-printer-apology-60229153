One line from a tired office printer, apologising to the people who keep
jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it — polite, resigned,
and done taking the blame. It apologises — then gets its own back, one
upside-down page at a time.

> "Sorry I jammed on page 47 of 200. You fed me paper thinner than a rumour, then blamed me. I finished it upside down."

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic) writes the entry to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
